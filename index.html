Features

Say it once. It’s already clean.

Works in every app

One shortcut, everywhere. Hush pastes into whatever has your cursor: chat, email, editor, terminal.

Hold or hands-free

Hold to talk and let go to send, or tap once to go hands-free and tap again to finish.

Cleans up filler words

Ums, false starts and repeats disappear. Punctuation and capitals are handled for you.

Personal dictionary & snippets

Teach it names, jargon and acronyms. Say a short cue and get the full text back.

Styles per context

Formal in email, relaxed in chat. Choose a tone for each kind of app you dictate into.

Private on-device mode

Run recognition locally and your voice never leaves your PC. No account needed.
